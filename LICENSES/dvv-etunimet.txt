CC BY 4.0 requires attribution and imposes no share-alike or
non-commercial condition, so it is compatible with distributing Decoy
under Apache-2.0. The attribution is discharged by naming DVV in NOTICE,
alongside the licence identifier and the URL the data came from.

Full licence text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

Decoy modifies the data: only the sheets counting names held as a first
given name are read, names are title-cased, and those with fewer than 200
bearers are dropped. CC BY 4.0 §3(a)(1)(B) requires modification to be
indicated, which the corpus provenance records and this paragraph states.
