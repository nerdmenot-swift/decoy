Full text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Its operative condition is attribution: a reuser must credit the source, indicate whether
changes were made, and link to the licence. Decoy discharges this through NOTICE, which
names the dataset, its version and the date it was retrieved, and which states plainly
that the corpus data has been modified -- filtered, re-encoded and deduplicated.

The dataset aggregates name frequencies from the civil registries of the United States,
United Kingdom, Canada and Australia.
