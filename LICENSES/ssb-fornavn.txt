CC BY 4.0 requires attribution and imposes no share-alike or
non-commercial condition, so it is compatible with distributing Decoy
under Apache-2.0. The attribution is discharged by naming Statistics
Norway in NOTICE, alongside the licence identifier and the source URL.

Full licence text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

Decoy modifies the data: names are title-cased, those below the threshold
are dropped, and the draw is re-weighted. CC BY 4.0 §3(a)(1)(B) requires
modification to be indicated, which the corpus provenance records and
this paragraph states.
