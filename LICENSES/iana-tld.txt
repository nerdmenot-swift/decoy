IANA operates the DNS root zone and publishes the list of delegated top-level
domains as a matter of record. Which strings are delegated is a fact, not an
authored work: there is no creative expression in the mapping and therefore
nothing to license.

Decoy takes only the delegated labels. It records IANA as the source, with the
root zone serial seen when the content was last verified.
