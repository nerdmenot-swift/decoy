In English: you may make use of the information commercially and
non-commercially.

The terms require attribution to the publishing body and impose no
share-alike and no non-commercial restriction, so they are compatible with
distributing Decoy under Apache-2.0. The attribution is discharged by
naming the Central Bureau of Statistics in NOTICE with the source URL.

One nuance stated rather than glossed: the State of Israel retains
copyright in the data. Decoy is licensed under Apache-2.0 and this data
travels with it under its own terms — Apache-2.0 governs Decoy's code and
does not require Decoy to own everything it ships. The data is not
relabelled Apache-2.0.

Carried in the source descriptor as `LicenseRef-DataGovIL-1.0` because
these terms have no SPDX short identifier.

Decoy modifies the data: the eight per-group tables are merged, only the
all-years total is read, and names below the threshold are dropped.
