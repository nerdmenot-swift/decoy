No licence text exists for this source, and that is a recorded conclusion
rather than an omission.

The Global LEI Foundation is the registration authority for ISO 20275,
"Financial Services -- Entity Legal Forms (ELF)", and publishes the code
list of entity legal forms in each jurisdiction. Decoy reads two facts
from it: which legal forms a jurisdiction has, and what each is
abbreviated to in the local language.

Both are facts about statute. A German company may be a Gesellschaft mit
beschraenkter Haftung, abbreviated GmbH, because the GmbH-Gesetz says so;
an Austrian Offene Gesellschaft is abbreviated OG because Austrian company
law says so. There is no creative expression in recording that, and no
authorship for anyone to license. GLEIF's own contribution -- assigning an
ELF code to each form, and the selection and arrangement of the list -- is
not used here.

GLEIF does grant CC0 in two nearby places, and this file is covered by
neither:

  - The Open Source page grants CC0 for GLEIF's software repositories.
  - The LEI Data Terms of Use grant CC0 for "data available through the
    Access Service", which is the LEI register.

The ISO 20275 code list is published separately from both, and its
download page states no licence of its own. Reading either grant as
covering it would be convenient and unsupported, so neither is claimed.

This is the same conclusion, on the same reasoning, that Decoy records for
the ISO 4217 currency registry published by SIX Group. Both are ISO code
lists maintained by a registration authority; both are read for the facts
they record rather than for the compilation.

Source: https://www.gleif.org/en/about-lei/code-lists/iso-20275-entity-legal-forms-code-list
