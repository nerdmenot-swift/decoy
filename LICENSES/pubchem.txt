Only the element name, symbol and atomic number are taken. Those are IUPAC
assignments — facts with a single correct answer — and PubChem is used because
IUPAC publishes them as prose and PDF with nothing machine-readable to fetch.

Note that PubChem aggregates data deposited by third parties, which can carry
their own terms. That does not reach the periodic table, which is why this source
is scoped to it.
