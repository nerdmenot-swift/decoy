WordNet-SALDO ships no licence file. Its only rights statement is the `dc:rights`
field of the OLAC metadata record reproduced below, which reads `CC-BY attribution`
-- naming neither a version nor a holder.

That is why this source is recorded as bare `CC-BY` rather than `CC-BY-3.0` or
`CC-BY-4.0`: pinning a version would assert terms the distributor never stated. The
attribution obligation is discharged by naming Sprakbanken in the provenance record
either way, since it is common to every CC BY version.
