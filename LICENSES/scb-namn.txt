CC BY 4.0 requires attribution and imposes no share-alike or
non-commercial condition, so it is compatible with distributing Decoy
under Apache-2.0.

SCB attaches one condition to how the attribution is worded, stated on
its terms page:

    "Om du själv bearbetar statistik från SCB:s webbplats får du inte
     ange SCB som källa."

In English: if you rework statistics from SCB's website yourself, you may
not cite SCB as the source. The reasoning is that SCB will not stand
behind arithmetic it did not do. The credit in that case should read
"based on data from Statistics Sweden" rather than "Source: SCB".

Decoy does rework the data — it reads the tilltalsnamn sheets rather than
the förnamn sheets, drops names with fewer than 200 bearers, title-cases
them, and re-weights the draw — so the second form is the correct one and
is what NOTICE carries.
