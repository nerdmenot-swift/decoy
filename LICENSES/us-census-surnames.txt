The 2010 Census surname file is a work of the United States Government, prepared
by officers or employees of the US Census Bureau as part of their official
duties. Under 17 U.S.C. 105 such works are not subject to copyright protection in
the United States, so there is no grant to reproduce and no attribution
obligation to discharge.

Decoy nonetheless records the source, its version and its retrieval date in the
corpus provenance, because being able to say where a value came from is useful
independently of whether anyone requires it.
