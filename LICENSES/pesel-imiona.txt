The Polish open data portal publishes this dataset under the Creative
Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication.

Confirmed from the portal's own API rather than from its web page:

    GET https://api.dane.gov.pl/1.4/datasets/1667

    "license_name": "CC0 1.0"
    "license_condition_custom_description": ""
    "license_condition_default_cc40": false
    "license_condition_db_or_copyrighted": ""
    "license_condition_personal_data": null

Every licence-condition field is empty, which is dane.gov.pl's way of
recording that the publisher attached no further terms.

Full text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

CC0 waives copyright and related rights to the fullest extent permitted by
law. There is no attribution condition and no share-alike condition, so
nothing here constrains distributing Decoy under Apache-2.0.

Decoy names the Ministry of Digital Affairs in NOTICE regardless of that,
because the provenance records exist to answer where a value came from
rather than only to discharge obligations.

Source: https://dane.gov.pl/pl/dataset/1667
