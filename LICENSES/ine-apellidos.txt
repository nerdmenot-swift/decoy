The INE states the licence for the statistical information on its website
at https://ine.es/dyngs/AYU/index.htm?cid=125:

    "La licencia de uso general a aplicar a la información estadística de
     este sitio web, salvo que se indique lo contrario, es la Creative
     Commons Reconocimiento 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)... incluso con finalidad
     comercial citando la autoría."

In English: the general licence applying to the statistical information on
this website, unless otherwise indicated, is Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 (CC BY 4.0) — including for commercial purposes, citing the author.

CC BY 4.0 requires attribution and imposes no share-alike or
non-commercial condition, so it is compatible with distributing Decoy
under Apache-2.0. The attribution is discharged by naming the INE in
NOTICE, alongside the licence identifier and the URL the data came from.

Full licence text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode

Decoy modifies the data: names are title-cased from the register's upper
case, and those borne by fewer than 200 people are dropped. CC BY 4.0
§3(a)(1)(B) requires modification to be indicated, which the corpus
provenance records and this paragraph state.
