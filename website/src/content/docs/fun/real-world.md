---
title: Real-world lists
description: Animals, food, mountains, books — and why these took longest to allow.
---

Animals, breeds, produce, cheeses, peaks, rivers, books, composers, universities, clubs.
These were refused for months on a rule stricter than its own reasoning: there *is* a
fact of the matter about whether an otter is an animal, so it wanted a citable source,
and no suitable one exists.

The conclusion was wrong. "Cannot be mechanically verified" is not "cannot be shipped" —
a fixture library that will not tell you an otter is an animal is not being rigorous.
What the discipline buys is knowing *which* data is checked, and that is preserved by
putting these under their own source whose descriptor says plainly that accuracy is high
and unverified.
